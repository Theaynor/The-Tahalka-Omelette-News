Gaurav Raped by his two friends “Movie Plan” turns into nightmare for Gaurav.

By Tahalka Staff | January 5, 2026 | Lucknow

A student allegedly lured under the pretext of a "movie project" was first assaulted and later found dead after accused were granted bail. Police form special team amid protests.

Lucknow, January 5, 2026 — A 20‑year‑old BBA student, identified as Gaurav Singh Kalakoti, was found dead in Neelmatha early this week, police say. According to the FIR filed at Cantonment Police Station and police sources, the incident followed an earlier alleged sexual assault in which Gaurav was reportedly lured by two acquaintances under the pretext of making a short film.

The FIR, lodged by relatives, states that on December 12, 2025, Gaurav was invited by two classmates who told him they were working on a "movie project". He was taken to a deserted lane in Neelmatha where, the report alleges, he was sexually assaulted. A medico-legal report from King George's Medical University (KGMU) reportedly documented injuries consistent with assault and confirmed presence of seminal material that matched two individuals, the FIR records say.

Police arrested both suspects after the first report; one identified in police records as Saurav Chamar, a former classmate from Army Public School (APS), SP Marg. The second accused remains unnamed in public records at present. Gaurav survived that initial assault and returned home, family members said. "Vo zinda tha, lekin bahut traumatized," a friend told this reporter on condition of anonymity.

In a development that has sparked public outrage, the arrested accused were reportedly released on bail on December 28, 2025, amid procedural disputes over evidence collection in court. Within days, Gaurav was found dead in the same Neelmatha lane, on January 2, 2026. Locals say the body was discovered early morning, wrapped and showing signs of a violent struggle. Police sources describe evidence that suggests he was assaulted again before his death — these are allegations under investigation.

Investigation and police response

Lucknow Police Commissioner has constituted a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to probe the incident. Officers say they are examining CCTV, mobile call records, and forensics. "We are treating this as a serious case of alleged sexual assault followed by suspicious death," a senior officer said on condition of anonymity because the investigation is ongoing.

The family of the deceased, who hail from a Pahadi village in Uttarakhand, have demanded a Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) probe, alleging local bias and pressure because of the social backgrounds of those involved. "Hamara beta padhne aaya tha. Ab hamaare sapne khatam ho gaye," Gaurav's father told reporters during a visit to Lucknow. The family requests privacy and a fair trial.

Campus reaction and protests

Students and activists staged protests outside KKC (Shri Jai Narain Misra Post Graduate College) and at parts of Lucknow demanding "Justice for Gaurav." Placards read "Justice, not bail" and "Protect students." Campus leaders called for immediate safety audits for outstation students and faster handling of sexual assault cases by colleges and police.

Legal context and concerns

Legal experts say the case highlights two worrying trends: underreporting of sexual violence against men and the consequences of procedural gaps in evidence collection. "Whether the accused should have been granted bail is a question for the courts," said a criminal law expert. "But the safety of victims must be paramount, and police procedures must be strengthened to prevent recurrence."

Background — who was Gaurav?

Gaurav, originally from the hills of Uttarakhand, had moved to Lucknow to pursue a BBA at KKC. He completed his 12th from Army Public School, SP Marg, in humanities and was described by peers as soft-spoken and focused on his studies. His Instagram handle, public at the time of reporting, is listed as @_gaurav.__.singh__ (profile details and images not reprinted here).

What the accused say

One accused, named in police records as Saurav Chamar, is back in custody; he has denied involvement in the murder but acknowledges knowing the deceased. The second accused remains at large; raids are ongoing in surrounding villages, police say. All accused are presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.

Community reaction and helpline

Community groups and migrant student organisations from the Pahadi community have called for better accommodation safety and a review of campus transportation and late‑night supervision policies. Police urge anyone with information to contact the Cantonment station or call 112.
