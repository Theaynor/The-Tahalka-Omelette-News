About The Tahalka Omelette News

The Tahalka Omelette News is an independent digital newsroom dedicated to local reporting, campus journalism and investigative features. We prioritise verified reporting and ethical journalism. We seek to report responsibly on sensitive issues while protecting legal rights and privacy.

Editorial policy (summary)

  • We report allegations as alleged and clearly identify sources.
  • We avoid graphic descriptions of sexual violence; we prioritise dignity of victims and families.
  • We verify images and refrain from publishing private material without consent.
